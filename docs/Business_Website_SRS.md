# Software Requirements Specification (SRS)

## Project Title

**Business Website for Road Construction Machinery & Industrial
Components Sourcing Company**

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. Introduction

## 1.1 Purpose

This document defines the software requirements for a professional B2B
corporate website for a company specializing in sourcing road
construction machinery, industrial automation products, electrical
control systems, and spare parts across multiple states in India.

## 1.2 Objectives

-   Establish a professional online presence.
-   Generate qualified business leads.
-   Showcase products and sourcing capabilities.
-   Build trust with contractors, infrastructure companies, and
    procurement teams.
-   Provide a scalable foundation for future digital services.

## 1.3 Project Scope

### Included (Version 1)

-   Corporate Website
-   Product Showcase
-   About Company
-   Contact & Inquiry Forms
-   Quote Request System
-   Mobile Responsive Design
-   SEO-Friendly Pages
-   Admin Dashboard (future implementation)

### Excluded (Version 1)

-   Online Payments
-   Shopping Cart
-   ERP Integration
-   Customer Portal
